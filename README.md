# Tailor App

A specialized digital management system tailored for boutique owners, independent tailors, and fashion designers to digitize their workflow, track orders, and maintain financial logs.

---

## System Overview

The Tailor App serves as a centralized platform to replace traditional paper registers. It is designed to track complex customer measurements, manage the lifecycle of custom clothing orders, and maintain a clear ledger of payments, advanced deposits, and outstanding balances.

---

## Core Modules and Capabilities

### 1. Customer Management System
* **Digital Directory:** Store and access client profiles, historical orders, and contact details from a unified list.
* **Measurement Vault:** Log detailed, anatomical measurements required for custom fitting (e.g., chest, waist, length, sleeves) linked permanently to the user profile for quick re-ordering.

### 2. Stitching Job Order Tracker
* **Lifecycle Pipeline:** Track every garment through distinct production phases: Booking, Cutting, Stitching, Fitting, and Ready for Delivery.
* **Deadline Management:** Monitor delivery schedules and pending tasks via a prioritized order queue to ensure on-time completion.

### 3. Financial Khata Ledger
* **Transaction Logging:** Replaces traditional physical ledgers with a digital bookkeeping system to record overall shop performance.
* **Balance Tracking:** Monitor down payments, calculate remaining balances upon delivery, and flag accounts with outstanding dues.
* **Earnings Analytics:** View daily and monthly revenue breakdowns directly from the shop dashboard.

---
